02 · the hook02 / 10 · v0.0.0-dev

Bu Sari is a composite scenario, not a real person. The point is structural: the dangerous state — nobody at the board — is the one a motion camera cannot see.

Absence is the event. That is what jaga is built to watch.

03 · the problem03 / 10 · v0.0.0-dev

The two cheap alternatives watch the wrong thing: one watches movement, the other watches the clock. Neither watches the state you care about.

Both are baselines in the pre-registered protocol — jaga will be compared with them under the same rule.

07 · the rule07 / 10 · v0.0.0-dev

Twelve windows, all above θ: k = ⌈0.8 × 12⌉ = 10, so it trips on the tenth. The decision rule is small enough to check exhaustively: every pattern of twelve windows, at four strides and three dwells, against a separate implementation.

One stray window can never cut the power.
