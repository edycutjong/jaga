jaga · built for the Nebius Global AI Hackathon, Physical AI track01 / 10 · v0.1.0 · untagged

jaga is Indonesian for "keep watch". It is built so that you type one sentence, a webcam on your laptop watches for it, and a cheap smart plug cuts the power when it comes true. So far it has run on recordings and fake plugs; the real hardware run is still to be recorded.

The terminal on the right is a real compile, captured on 17 October: an Indonesian sentence in, English captions back.

An empty room has no motion.

A scenario: Bu Sari leaves the iron on and runs to the warung. Her CCTV stays silent.

Bu Sari is a composite scenario, not a real person. The point is structural: the dangerous state, nobody at the board, is the one a motion camera cannot see.

Absence is the event. That is what jaga is built to watch.

03 · the problem03 / 10 · v0.1.0 · untagged

The two cheap alternatives watch the wrong thing: one watches movement, the other watches the clock. Neither watches the state you care about.

A stillness timer (the motion camera's logic, turned around) and a plug countdown timer are both baselines in the pre-registered protocol, scored by the same rule as jaga.

07 · the rule07 / 10 · v0.1.0 · untagged

Ten windows, all above θ: k = ⌈0.8 × 10⌉ = 8, so it trips on the eighth. The decision rule is small enough to check exhaustively: every pattern of twelve windows, at four strides and three dwells, against a separate implementation.

One stray window can never cut the power.
