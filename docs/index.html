One window never cuts the power.

The model chooses where to look; the code decides when to act. By default a trip needs k = ⌈0.8 n⌉ of the n windows in the dwell above θ, the newest included, and never fewer than two. Flip the windows and watch the rule.

04Acttinytuya · Shelly RPC
OFF or notify, never ONTested on fakes

A trip can only switch it off.

  • I1A trip can only cut power or notify. The one ON path, jaga/operator_on.py, is reached only by operator actions; tests check nothing else reaches it.
  • I2One bad window cannot cut power: most windows over the dwell, the newest included, never fewer than two.
  • I3OFF is shown only after the plug acknowledges this trip's OFF; a failed call shows ACTION FAILED and is retried, off only.

Power can be cut only for heating or cooking appliances; a fridge or a freezer never. Neither plug driver has met a real plug yet: in the tests, both run against fakes.

06Measureclips · soaks · outside people
clips and soaks pre-registeredPending

Pending is a state, not a number.

Accuracy needs recordings only a person can make. The protocol was committed first, question and target included:

“Of the still episodes in Soak B1 + B2, how many does jaga cut, and how many does a stillness timer cut — judged by one decision rule — and how many real exits does each catch, how fast?”

Target: under half the stillness timer's cuts, and at least 0.8 of the exits caught. Otherwise the counts are read out with no claim of a win. A tie is called a tie.

Read the labels before you wire it.

Three limits, quoted from the README as written. A judge should know them before believing anything above.

Questions a judge would ask.

Does any video leave the laptop?+

No. The typed sentence goes to Token Factory; labelled margins (no vectors) go to the calibration Job; an optional push, off unless you turn it on, sends one text line per alert. The overlay listens on 127.0.0.1. An offline check refuses every connection outside the home network, then trips a tripwire on stand-in window vectors and switches the fake plug OFF. A Tuya plug's own firmware may call Tuya's cloud; the Shelly path can be fully local.

How accurate is it?+

Not measured yet. The clips, two attended soaks and one night are pre-registered in docs/PROTOCOL.md, committed before any data. An accuracy number appears here only once bench.py writes it to DEMO.md.

Is anything on this page mocked?+

The overlay pictures are QA renders (the real app on scripted windows, a fake plug and a test card) and say so. The breaker at the top is a drawing of the rule. The terminal is typed back from real transcripts. Every measured number links its file.

Can it tell whether the iron is hot?+

No. At 224p jaga sees scene states (a person there or not, a door open), not an iron's power light. So tripwire 01 watches for nobody at the board.

What stops a wrong trip from doing harm?+

A trip can only cut power or notify (I1, tested); one window never trips (I2, all 49,152 patterns); OFF shows only on the plug's acknowledgement (I3). Only heating or cooking appliances can be cut, and a person switches power back on.

Why does Nemotron rewrite my sentence?+

The Cosmos-Embed1 card says prompts unlike its short action captions may not align with video. Super writes that register, plus hard negatives that make “nobody” a visual contrast. What that is worth is a planned ablation (DEMO.md §5), not run yet.
